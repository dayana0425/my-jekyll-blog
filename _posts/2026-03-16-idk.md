## Who Am I?

Well, I am 26 (27 in April) and currently working as a substitute teacher at different schools. I am no where where I thought I'd be in life right now and part of that makes me feel sad and like I failed.
I got laid off from my Software Engineering job in December 2025. I've had several interviews, several rejections, and ultimately no offers yet. The tech landscape feels tough and unforgiving.
I thought I would have never worked any job except for tech, but here I am.
It's crazy how things can change so quickly.
I went through a period of grief and maybe I am still in that period...
but I do feel like I am no longer tied to the identity I used to have which was basically a 'tech bro' even though I'm a woman and latina... I always felt like the identity I had was a tech bro which sounds awful!

Even though it's been a tough journey, I think I've made progress in myself... I feel I am more myself. 
I feel I am closer to who I want to be. Who I always wanted to be.
The only thing missing is 'THE JOB'.

Don't get me wrong, I still want to work in tech, but I felt like BEFORE, tech was my whole world and my everything. I knew nothing else.
Now, I know a whole new world. Since I was laid off, I got to spend a lot of time with myself and focus on my goals outside of my career. 
I focused on my friendships, my family, my health, my hobbies, and my personal growth.
I also work as a substitute teacher and working with children makes me feel like WOW, I remember being that age... and how optimistic I was about the world and thinking that if only I study hard and work hard... I can be a Software Engineer... all my problems will be solved! I'll buy my dad a truck and my mom a house.
I laugh now because life is not so straight forward but ALSO, I feel like I am more myself and I am more confident in who I am and what I want.

## What Now?

Not sure!!! I am still figuring it out but I'm grateful to be healthy and alive.

## Conclusion

Thanks for reading my garbage and hopefully it didn't come off as too dramatic or sad. This is just how I currently feel.
